My Stance on AI

Anyone who knows me knows my vehement moral opposition to generative AI. That shouldn't be a hot take at this point, but there's a widespread culture which has sprung up out of unfortunate necessity that implicates all creatives as a target of suspicion. I write this manifesto to dispel any and all ambiguity in regards to my position and to solidify my allyship with human creativity on the indie web (and in general).

Let it be known hereon that ROBGARAGE has never, and will never, intentionally use generative AI in any capacity whatsoever.

All art, writing, and code on my website was made with love by human hands.

While I do my very best to vet the authenticity of any images and resources that I use here, I'm only human and mistakes will be made. There's a nonzero chance that certain things may slip through the cracks, especially with how parasitically entangled AI has dug its roots. Please contact me ASAP if you notice I've accidentally used something AI generated so I can take it down immediately. I do not and will never endorse generative AI, and I wish to keep it far away from all of my creative endeavors.

If you are someone who passionately uses and defends generative AI, respectfully, my website is not for you. I urge you to educate yourself on its harms and allow yourself the pleasure of making something yourself, not letting a robot do it for you, and only then return. You can only outsource your thinking so much until it stops being yours.

I strongly believe that every human is an artist -- regardless of race, gender, age, disability, or skill -- and that includes you, my friend! So go make something without a soulless bot holding you back. I know it's hard. Art taps into the most primitive part of our souls, and that level of vulnerability with yourself can be terrifying. But trust me, if I've learned anything in my almost two decades of actively creating, it's that it's worth it. You have something to say too. I believe in you. You've got this.
