"What brushes do you use?"

...is undoubtedly the most frequent art-related question I receive, but because I'm awful at keeping track of where I download them from, I've never been able to give a satisfactory answer... until NOW!!!!!!! This page exists to remedy that quandary and archive the various custom brushes I use for my digital art.

Keep in mind most of these will not work across every program. Procreate brushes will likely only work on Procreate, CSP brushes will likely only work on CSP, and so on. There is probably a way to convert a certain brush from one program to another, but unless you're willing to figure out how to do that, you're better off finding (or making) an equivalent for your program of choice.

One additional important note: I've tried my very best to trace these brushes back to their original sources, but since I've had many of them for years and (foolishly) never saved where I found them, brushes whose creators I could not locate are marked with an (X). If you or someone you know made a brush(set) marked (X), please contact me so I can give proper credit!!!!

Brushes I've Made

All of these are available to download on my Kofi for free, though tips are greatly appreciated :-)

  • Prickle - Brush with a prickly texture. I made it because I found this awesome CSP brush and wanted something similar to use on Procreate. Very fun to sketch and ink with.
